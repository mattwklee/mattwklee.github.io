Our research spans the full electric energy conversion chain - from wide-bandgap (WBG) power semiconductor devices, through advanced converter and inverter topologies, to electric machines and their integration - with applications in transportation electrification, aerospace, distributed energy resources, and AI data centers. Current and past sponsors include NSF (CAREER), DOE ARPA-E (IGNIITE), DOE Vehicle Technologies Office, USDA NRCS, US Army DEVCOM GVSC, NASA, the Silicon Crossroads Microelectronics Commons Hub, BorgWarner, Hyundai Motors, General Motors, and others.

### Neutral-Point-Less (NPL) Multilevel Inverters

Multilevel inverters improve efficiency and power quality but traditionally require split DC-link capacitors and complex voltage balancing. Our NPL inverter family (H-type and X-type) achieves three-level operation with a single DC-link capacitor, eliminating the neutral point entirely. Ongoing work covers active common-mode voltage cancellation, DC-link ripple characterization, hybrid Si/SiC realizations, and GaN-based hardware with optimized power loop layout for traction and propulsion applications.

### Rare-Earth-Free Electric Machines

Rare-earth permanent magnets pose supply chain, cost, and sustainability challenges. We design high-power density wound-field synchronous machines and wound-field flux-switching machines that eliminate rare-earth magnets without sacrificing performance, including modular rotor structures, torque ripple mitigation, and magnetically integrated drives. This thrust is supported by the NSF CAREER award and connects to a broader circular-economy vision for reuse, repurposing, and recycling in transportation electrification.
</div>

### Optical Power Transfer and OPUS Generators

Delivering power across an isolation barrier or onto a spinning rotor with light enables entirely new machine and converter architectures. Under the ARPA-E IGNIITE program (and its 2026 Director's Award plus-up), we are developing Optically Powered Ultra-high-Speed (OPUS) wound-field synchronous generators with contactless optical field excitation, as well as optically powered and optically communicating gate drives for series-connected medium-voltage SiC devices.
</div>

### Partial Discharge and EMI in WBG Motor Drives

Fast-switching WBG inverters stress machine insulation with high dv/dt voltage pulses, causing partial discharge (PD) and electromagnetic interference (EMI) that threaten drive reliability. We develop measurement techniques, high-frequency models, and mitigation strategies - from voltage-based PD signature analysis to skin-effect-based suppression - spanning conditions from aerospace altitude to high temperature. Our work in this area received the IEEE IAS Industrial Drives Committee Transactions First Paper Award.
</div>

### Single-Stage Matrix Converters for DC Fast Charging and V2G

Direct AC-DC conversion without a bulky intermediate DC link enables compact, efficient DC fast chargers and vehicle-to-grid (V2G) interfaces. We investigate single-stage indirect matrix converter topologies with dual-gate bidirectional switches, novel carrier-based and translation-based modulation, reactive power compensation, and protection circuits.
</div>

### Solar Microinverters for Smart Agriculture

In partnership with USDA NRCS and Michigan State University, we develop retrofittable shunt-connected solar microinverters that soft-start irrigation pump motors and provide active/reactive power support, improving water and energy use efficiency for growers. The concept has been patented and field-oriented, with supporting work on low-cost sensing and grid synchronization.
</div>

### Electrified Aerial Mobility and Electric Drones

Lightweight, high-power density electric propulsion is the key constraint for drones and urban air mobility. We design high-speed machines, integrated motor drives, and laser-based optical power delivery for sustained flight, with support from NASA and industry partners, and through student teams including the GoAERO Prize Purdue team.
</div>

### Power Delivery for AI Data Centers

Exploding AI compute demand is redefining power delivery from the grid to the point of load. We study high-efficiency DCX voltage regulators, vertical power delivery, and rare-earth-free integrated motor drives for data center cooling, in collaboration with industry and international partners.
